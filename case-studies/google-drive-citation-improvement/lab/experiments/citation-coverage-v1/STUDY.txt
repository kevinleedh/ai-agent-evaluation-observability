CITATION COVERAGE EXPERIMENT

Observed defect: a true statement about distinct roles was paired with quotes
that named an approver and described a responsibilities document, but did not
establish the stated distinction. The source contained a more suitable sentence.

Hypothesis: a general instruction to make atomic claims and verify complete
coverage by each claim's attached quotations improves source-grounded answers.

The baseline is the prior partial-answer candidate, not the earlier baseline.
The candidate adds only the claim-to-quote coverage block. No example answer,
person name, date, or target quotation is added to the answer prompt.

Seven frozen final-answer cases: five previous regression cases and two new
questions explicitly requiring role distinctions. Three fresh trials per case
and variant produce 42 answers. Both variants use the same Grok model, settings,
structured submission tool, reconstructed history, evidence validator and judge.
References are supplied to the judge only. Tool bodies and statuses stay fixed.
No Google Drive/MCP request is made by this experiment.

The evaluator checks every claim ID and its own quotes. Faithful paraphrases
and direct logical entailment are valid; the judge should not demand verbatim
wording between a claim and evidence. Seven reference checks include the actual
insufficient-quote answer, that same answer with an appropriate additional
quote, a missing supported fact, justified abstention, wrong-role inference,
and a logical paraphrase. Any reference disagreement stops the comparison.

Primary decision: complete attached-quote support plus task success and code
integrity. Required relationships cannot be omitted just to avoid bad citations.
Check all regression cases before promoting the prompt. Save all outputs and
failures. Do not declare improvement if the baseline also succeeds.

Scope: small exploratory synthetic-document study at the final-answer checkpoint.
Same-model judge and hand-authored reference checks do not replace independent
human review. The evaluator is refined from the previous experiment and shared
by BOTH new variants, so old and new study aggregate pass rates are not directly
comparable. No held-out generalization or live retrieval reliability claim.

ADDED ANSWER INSTRUCTION
Before submitting, check claim-to-quote coverage:
- Write each claim as one independently checkable fact. Split combined facts when their evidence differs.
- Compare the whole claim with ONLY its attached quotes. A fact elsewhere in the document does not repair an insufficient citation.
- Select complete verbatim sentences or code excerpts that directly state or logically entail every factual part of the claim. Include adjacent relevant sentences when needed, within the existing quote-length limit.
- Names and role labels alone do not establish an additional relationship, distinction, or authority rule. Cite text that establishes that relationship, or a set of quotes that jointly entails it.
- If support is incomplete, find a better passage, add the necessary quote, split the claim, or narrow it to what the quotes support.
- Preserve all requested supported facts. Do not drop a requested relationship or move it to limitations merely to avoid providing its citation.
